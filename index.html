🎯 What Is browser-agent?

browser-agent is a smart assistant that lives right inside your Chrome browser. Think of it as having a helpful friend in a side panel that can see what you're looking at and help you with it.

  • 💬 Reads your tab - understands the content you're viewing
  • ⚡ Works alongside you - takes actions in your browser
  • 🔒 Your data stays yours - no cloud server involved
  • 🔓 Completely free - open source under MIT license

🏠 Run Locally With Ollama or LM Studio

🚀 Getting Started

Ready to supercharge your browsing? Here's how to get started in minutes:

📥 Step 1: Download browser-agent

Visit this link to download the application

🛠️ Step 2: Add to Chrome

Once downloaded, open Chrome and go to chrome://extensions. Turn on "Developer mode" in the top right corner, then click "Load unpacked" and select the folder you downloaded.

🔑 Step 3: Connect Your AI Provider

In the extension settings, choose your provider:

  • Cloud options: Enter your API key for Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, or OpenRouter
  • Local options: Select Ollama or LM Studio (make sure they're running on your computer)

🎉 Step 4: Start Using It!

Click the browser-agent icon in your toolbar to open the side panel. You're ready to go!

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to be a programmer to use this?

Absolutely not! We designed browser-agent for everyone. If you can use Chrome, you can use browser-agent. No coding knowledge needed.

Is my data private?

Yes! Since there's no server involved, your data stays on your computer. It only goes to whatever AI provider you choose to connect.

What does "MIT License" mean?

It means browser-agent is completely free to use, modify, and share. It's open source, so anyone can see how it works and contribute to making it better.

Will this work on any computer?

browser-agent works on any computer that runs Google Chrome. That includes Windows, Mac, and Linux systems. It's lightweight and doesn't need much power.
